## ✅ 06 | Acceptance Criteria

### US-19: Share Opened Time Capsules

- A user can share an opened capsule with another registered user.
- The recipient can view the shared memory.
- The recipient cannot edit, delete, or reshare the capsule.
- The owner can remove sharing access.
- Unauthorized users cannot access the shared memory.

### US-20: Search Time Capsules

- A user can search capsules by title.
- Search works regardless of letter capitalization.
- The search displays matching capsules.
- Search can be cleared to restore the collection.
