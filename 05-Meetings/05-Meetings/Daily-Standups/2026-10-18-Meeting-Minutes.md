## 📝 03 | Meeting Summary

During our first team meeting, we discussed the development of our project, Time Capsule – Digital Memories for the Future.

We reviewed the main idea of the application and discussed how users would be able to preserve personal memories and access them at a selected future date.

We also discussed the importance of teamwork, clear communication, and organizing our development activities.

The team agreed to use GitHub to manage project documentation, share source code, track changes, and collaborate throughout development.
