
# Sprint Reviews and Retrospectives – Time Digital Capsule

## Sprint 1 – Review

**Sprint Goal:** Establish the basic application structure, user registration, login, and database connectivity.

### Completed Work
- Developed the welcome page.
- Implemented registration and login functionality.
- Established initial database integration.
- Began dashboard development.

### Product Owner Review
The initial authentication functionality was demonstrated. Dashboard and additional features were carried forward for further development.

## Sprint 1 – Retrospective

**What Went Well**
- Team roles and Scrum responsibilities were established.
- Initial authentication features were developed.
- The team began coordinating development and documentation.

**Challenges**
- Some functionality remained incomplete during the first sprint.
- Additional integration and testing were required.

**Improvements**
- Define clearer acceptance criteria.
- Allocate sufficient time for testing and integration.

## Sprint 2 – Retrospective

**What Went Well**
- Capsule creation and draft management were completed.

- Testing provided evidence for acceptance.

**Challenges**

**Improvements**

- Maintain consistent documentation of testing results.

## Sprint 3 – Review

**Sprint Goal:** Implement time-based capsule sealing and unlocking.

### Completed Work
- Implemented future opening date and time selection.
- Added capsule sealing.
- Prevented access before the scheduled opening.
- Implemented countdown functionality.
- Enabled unlocking at the scheduled time.
- Completed 7 of 7 sprint tasks and 9 documented tests.

### Product Owner Review
Sprint 3 functionality and regression testing were completed and accepted.

## Sprint 3 – Retrospective

**What Went Well**
- Time-based capsule functionality was completed.
- Previous registration, login, and draft features continued working.
- All documented tests passed.

**Challenges**
- Time-based access restrictions required careful testing.

**Improvements**
- Test boundary conditions for scheduled functionality.
- Continue regression testing after major changes.

---

## Sprint 4 – Final Review

**Sprint Goal:** Complete sharing, recipient permissions, capsule search, testing, and final documentation.

### Completed Work
- Implemented sharing of unlocked capsules with authorized recipients.
- Added recipient access and read-only permissions.
- Implemented capsule search by title.
- Updated system modeling and development documentation.
- Completed 10 of 10 sprint tasks and 11 documented checks.
- Completed regression testing of earlier sprint functionality.

### Product Owner Review
Sprint 4 deliverables were tested and accepted. The four-sprint development cycle was completed.

## Sprint 4 – Final Retrospective

**What Went Well**
- The final sprint tasks were completed.
- Features from earlier sprints passed regression testing.
- Scrum artifacts, testing reports, and meeting records were organized.

**Challenges**
- Sharing permissions and integration required careful verification.
- Keeping backlog statuses and testing documentation consistent required attention.

**Improvements and Lessons Learned**
- Verify acceptance criteria throughout each sprint.
- Maintain updated documentation alongside development.
- Reserve dedicated time for final integration and regression testing.
- Continue clear communication between the Product Owner, Scrum Master, and developers.
